1973

At Stanford University, while studying deep space spectrographs, a breakthrough was made, from data recieved from Makarian 231. Our companies founders discovered a secret to opening doors in spacetime using the power of magnetism. This allowed them to create the worlds first SRA, and soon thereafter, they managed to open a wormhole.

Soon, their experiments yielded an efficient, and very rapid method of shipping packages. Today we have 7 facilities worldwide which ship not only to one another, but to any doorstep on the planet, instantly, and with ease. Our founders named this company Quazar, in honor of the source of their discovery.

Core Leadership Principles

Here at Quazar, we believe in a customer-first experience. That means no hidden fees, no cancellations, and no delays*. Every package is hand delivered by our team members, right to it's destination. We also believe every customer has a right to transparency, and that means you get notified as soon as your package is delivered.
