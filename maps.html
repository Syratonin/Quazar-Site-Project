Geer Shipping Center

Named for one of our founders, the first ever shipping center to open, located right in San Francisco, and proudly serving most of North America, Geer Shipping Center handles nearly 2 million orders every day!

Lockwood Logistics

Lockwood Distribution is our newest branch, utilizing experimental technology which can only be consistently cooled in the remote reaches of Siberia, very few packages are actually handled here, and its primary function is the developing of more energy efficient technology, and data processing for our sister sites accross the globe

Eastern Shores Distribution

Partnered with a local tech startup and located in Australia, Eastern Shores handles most of our Oceania customers, and has also singlehandedly boosted internet connectivity in the region.

Northen Mountain Mail

Quazar built this facility in 2004, servicing most of Asia, and reducing shipping costs for nearby production facilites nearly in twain. Located in northern India, a location chosen for it's proximity to multiple industrious nations.

Swiss Shippers

Proudly serving all of Europe, our first sister location was the first to have a dedicated nuclear plant just to produce it's own energy! Eventually, this practice become standard construction, as our volume has grown.

Mozambique Tumbling

Named after the country it is located in, this facility routes packages to and from all over Africa. Local water supplies have been greatly improved by the needed influx of purification plants brough about by our presence here.

Port Selene

Inspired by a beloved artist and located in Brazil, this site ships packages for all of South and Central America, and even has their own football team! (For our customers hailing from the US, thats soccer) Thus far, a total of over 500 million packages have been sent from this location alone!
